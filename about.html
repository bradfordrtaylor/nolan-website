About Nolan

AI built for the real work of capital markets

Nolan builds AI for the most manual parts of capital markets transactions: comfort circles and backup requests.

"The goal is simple. Automate the mechanics so lawyers can focus on judgment."

About Nolan

In every securities offering, lawyers spend hours identifying financial figures, circling statements, and coordinating backup with auditors.

The work is essential to comfort letters and diligence. Yet the process has remained largely manual for decades.

Teams still rely on spreadsheets, tracked changes, and manual review.

Nolan automates that workflow.

Upload an offering document or SEC filing and Nolan identifies the financial figures, places the circles, and organizes the backup requests lawyers and auditors rely on during diligence.

Why We Built Nolan

Bradford Taylor and Harris Scott met at Howard University School of Law and later worked as capital markets associates advising on securities offerings.

Across IPOs, follow on offerings, and debt transactions, they repeatedly saw the same process consume enormous associate time.

Lawyers manually identified financial figures across offering documents, circled them for comfort letters, and tracked backup requests across multiple parties.

The process was repetitive, high stakes, and largely unchanged.

After years doing the work themselves, they started building Nolan to fix it.

Nolan is designed specifically for capital markets lawyers and built around how diligence actually happens on real transactions.
