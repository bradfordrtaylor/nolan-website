Financial Figure Identification

Upload an offering document or other SEC filing. Nolan identifies financial figures typically included in comfort requests: dollar amounts, percentages, ratios, and share counts.

Precision Figure Circling

Nolan automatically places circles around financial figures directly in the PDF. Circles are placed tightly around the numbers so the output mirrors how lawyers prepare comfort requests for auditors.

Filing Coverage

Nolan works across offering documents and incorporated SEC filings including S-1s, 10-Ks, 10-Qs, prospectus supplements, and other incorporated filings.

Auditor Ready Output

Export a marked PDF showing every circled figure exactly where it appears in the document. The output can be used directly as a draft comfort request to send to auditors.

Precedent Learning

Upload a marked comfort request from a prior deal. Nolan learns which figures were circled and applies that logic when reviewing new filings.

Document Review Assistant

Ask questions about the filing in natural language. Show all circled figures, show only dollar amounts, hide percentages, or apply custom filters.

Statement Identification

Nolan reads the full filing and identifies statements that typically require backup support. Examples include employee counts, market position claims, and other factual statements about the company.

Full Statement Highlighting

Unlike comfort circles which mark individual numbers, backup highlights the full statement so the underlying claim can be supported with evidence.

Backup Request Tracker

Every highlighted statement is exported into a structured tracker with page references and request numbers. This mirrors the tracker lawyers create when sending backup requests to the company.

Linked Filing Viewer

Click any request in the tracker and Nolan jumps directly to the highlighted statement in the filing.

Evidence Upload

Upload supporting documents from the company and link them directly to the corresponding backup request.

Draft Comparison

When new drafts of the filing are uploaded, Nolan identifies new or changed statements so backup requests stay up to date across revisions. This mirrors the real diligence workflow where lawyers review redlines to identify new statements that require backup.

ACME CORP · FORM 10-Q BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Business Overview

Acme Corporation is a global manufacturer and distributor of explosive products, demolition equipment, and specialty propulsion systems. The Company operates through two reportable segments: Consumer Explosives and Industrial Solutions. For the three months ended September 30, 2024, consolidated net revenues were $178.3 million, an increase of 21.8% compared to the prior year period. As of September 30, 2024, we employed 4,812 full-time equivalent employees across 12 offices in North America and Europe. Total compensation expense for the quarter was $47.3 million, reflecting annual merit increases effective July 2024.

Our Consumer Explosives segment generated net revenues of $121.4 million for the quarter, an increase of 23.6% compared to the prior year period. We are the largest supplier of dynamite and controlled demolition products in North America, holding an estimated 34% market share as of the date of this filing. Gross margins in the Industrial Solutions segment improved to 42.8% from 38.1% in the year-ago quarter, driven primarily by supply chain optimization and raw material cost reductions.

We attribute our continued growth to longstanding client relationships and consistent product quality. Our customer retention rate has exceeded 95% for the past eight consecutive fiscal years. Revenue per active customer increased to approximately $248,000 during the quarter, up from $219,000 in the comparable prior year period. Operating expenses totaled $134.9 million, compared to $112.1 million in the prior year period.

Capital expenditures during the quarter were $18.7 million, primarily directed toward expansion of our Tucson manufacturing facility. We maintain exclusive distribution agreements with over 200 licensed contractors and government agencies across 14 countries. Management believes these relationships provide a significant competitive advantage in securing large-scale project contracts.
