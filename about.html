About Nolan

Nolan is building AI for the most manual parts of capital markets transactions.

In every securities offering, lawyers spend hours identifying financial figures, circling statements, and coordinating backup with auditors for comfort letters. The process is critical to the deal but has remained largely unchanged for decades.

Despite advances across the legal industry, comfort and backup preparation still happens through spreadsheets, tracked changes, and manual review.

Nolan automates that workflow.

Upload an offering document or SEC filing and Nolan identifies financial figures, places circles, and organizes the backup requests that lawyers and auditors rely on during diligence.

The goal is simple. Let lawyers focus on judgment instead of document mechanics.

Why We Built Nolan

Bradford Taylor and Harris Scott met at Howard University School of Law and later worked as capital markets associates advising on securities offerings.

Across IPOs, follow on offerings, and debt transactions, they repeatedly saw the same process consume enormous associate time.

Every deal required lawyers to manually identify financial figures across offering documents, circle them for comfort letters, and track backup requests across multiple parties.

The workflow was repetitive, high stakes, and entirely manual.

While other parts of dealmaking evolved with technology, this process remained largely unchanged.

After years doing the work themselves, they started building Nolan to fix it.

Nolan is designed specifically for capital markets lawyers and built around the way diligence actually happens on real transactions.
