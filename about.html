About Nolan

Purpose-built AI designed for capital markets lawyers. We're making the comfort letter and backup request workflow faster, more accurate, and more collaborative.

Why We're Building This

Bradford Taylor and Harris Scott met at Howard University School of Law, where Bradford mentored Harris. Both went on to work as capital markets associates at top law firms, spending years on securities offerings.

They saw firsthand how the comfort letter and backup preparation process consumed hundreds of associate hours per deal. The same manual workflow, repeated on every IPO, every follow-on, every debt deal.

Every other part of the deal process had been touched by technology. But comfort and backup preparation remained stuck in spreadsheets and tracked changes.

They started building Nolan to fix that. Purpose-built AI that understands how capital markets lawyers actually work.
