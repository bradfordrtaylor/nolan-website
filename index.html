Products

Two tools. One platform.
Every diligence workflow covered.

Nolan AI ships two purpose-built products — one for comfort letter preparation, one for backup verification. Same platform, same security, different workflows.

AI Figure Identification

Upload a 10-Q and Claude identifies every financial figure — dollar amounts, percentages, ratios, share counts — with precise PDF coordinates.

Smart Comfort Logic

Each figure is classified as CIRCLE or EXCLUDE with 15 exclusion codes: forward-looking, flash numbers, market data, approximate language, and more.

Review Assistant

Chat with your document. Ask to hide forward-looking statements, keep only dollar amounts, or apply deal-specific instructions — all in natural language.

Precedent Deals

Upload a marked-up PDF from a previous deal and Nolan learns your firm's comfort approach — which figures to circle, which to exclude, and why.

One-Click Export

Export a fully marked-up PDF with circled figures, ready for the auditor. Every circle has precise positioning that matches the original filing.

Filing-Aware Prompts

Different SEC filings require different analysis. Nolan ships dedicated prompt registries for 10-Qs, 10-Ks, S-1s, and prospectuses — not a generic one-size-fits-all.

Statement Identification

Claude reads every in-scope section and identifies statements of fact: headcount claims, facility counts, regulatory statements, contract terms — anything the company must prove.

Auto-Generated Tracker

Every identified statement is automatically exported to a structured Excel tracker with request numbers, page references, categories, and status columns.

Full-Sentence Highlighting

Unlike comfort where individual figures are circled, backup boxes entire sentences because the auditor and client need the full context of each factual claim.

In-App Tracker

A live interactive grid linked to the PDF viewer. Click any request row and the filing scrolls to that exact statement. Track status, add notes, filter by category.

Client Portal

Give clients scoped access to upload evidence documents against specific requests. Both sides see the same live tracker — no more emailing spreadsheets back and forth.

AI-Assisted Tie-Out

Claude reads the evidence documents and pre-verifies whether they support the claims. Lawyers shift from manual review to exception-based review.

How It Works

From filing to deliverable in minutes

A hybrid AI pipeline combines Azure Document Intelligence for pixel-perfect text extraction with Claude for financial reasoning. Two layers, zero hallucinations.

01

Upload

Drop an SEC filing into Nolan. Attach a precedent deal for context, or provide deal-specific instructions.

02

Extract

Azure Document Intelligence reads every page — text, tables, positions. Every word gets a precise bounding box.

03

Analyze

Claude classifies each element using filing-specific prompts and your deal preferences. Circle or exclude. Include or skip.

04

Review & Export

Review the marked-up filing, refine with the Review Assistant, and export a deal-ready PDF in one click.

Security

Built for the most sensitive
work in finance

Law firms audit vendors before signing. Nolan is built to pass. Every architectural decision prioritizes data isolation, encryption, and auditability.

AES-256 Encryption

All data encrypted at rest and in transit. TLS 1.2+ for all connections, AES-256-GCM for stored data. No exceptions.

Zero Training Guarantee

Your documents are never used to train AI models. We enforce this contractually with every AI provider. Inference only.

SOC 2 Roadmap

SOC 2 Type I certification targeted within 12 months of launch. Type II follows. Annual pen testing from day one.

Asymmetric Auth

RS256 JWT signing with 15-minute access tokens. Refresh token rotation, account lockout, and SSO hooks for enterprise identity providers.

Audit Logging

Every action logged with full audit trail — who accessed what, when, and from where. Append-only, 7-year retention, ADMIN-only access.

Org-Level Isolation

Every database query filters by organization. No cross-tenant data access is physically possible. Your data is yours alone.
